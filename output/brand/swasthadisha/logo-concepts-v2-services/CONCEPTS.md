# SwasthaDisha: service-led logo concepts

Tagline: **Healthcare without Boundaries**

Revised direction: visibly communicate doctor consultations, pharmacy, lab tests and hospital care through recognizable symbols and service names. All four concepts retain the brand name and tagline.

1. **Four-service Care Cross**: four service pictograms combined in one compact blue/teal mark.
2. **Connected Clinical Circle**: stethoscope surrounding medicine, laboratory and hospital symbols.
3. **Care Home with Service Strip**: open clinic doorway plus four labeled service icons.
4. **Clinical Wordmark**: stethoscope integrated with the initial S, with four labeled services.

Design preference: option 1 most directly incorporates all four services into the primary symbol. These are PNG concepts on white backgrounds; no production vector identity has been selected.
