# SwasthaDisha logo concepts

Tagline: **Healthcare without Boundaries**

Five original raster concept directions, with a readable wordmark, clear shapes and a global visual tone for healthcare access in smaller cities. Each PNG has a white background.

1. **Care Compass** — teal directional mark; finding a path to healthcare.
2. **Human Embrace** — teal support around a coral human figure; care centred on people.
3. **Open Horizon** — teal open path and amber sunrise; accessible care and optimism.
4. **Continuous Care** — indigo and teal connected ribbons; continuity across care services.
5. **SD Monogram** — blue initials with a forward path; a compact brand signature.

Design preference: Human Embrace for warmth and immediate human meaning; Care Compass for guidance and the Disha story. These are visual concept PNGs. A selected direction can be developed into a production vector identity with icon-only and monochrome variants.
