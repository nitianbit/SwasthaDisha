# Jansetu healthcare and business operations

Jansetu supports a patient's care journey and each participating business's private daily operations. These terms distinguish care, commerce and marketplace participation.

## Care and business operations

**Business**: The organization accountable for its own staff, private customers, services and commercial records. A business may operate several healthcare services.
_Avoid_: Using vendor, hospital, corporate customer or app user as universal synonyms.

**Branch**: A business's operating location, with its own schedules, service availability and stores.

**Department**: A clinical or operational unit within a business, such as OPD, laboratory or pharmacy. A department is not automatically an independent business.

**Patient**: The person receiving care, whether or not they have a phone or app account.
_Avoid_: Treating patient and login user as interchangeable.

**Customer**: A person or business buying a product or service. A clinical service identifies the patient separately from the payer or buyer.

**Individual customer**: A person buying care, tests or medicines outside an employer-sponsored corporate arrangement. The buyer may differ from the patient receiving care.

**Caregiver**: A person authorized to act for a patient within a recorded scope.

**Encounter**: A particular clinical interaction, with its responsible practitioner and clinical record.

**Episode**: A related set of care activities spanning one or more encounters, tests and follow-ups.

**Appointment**: A reserved opportunity to receive a service. It is distinct from a completed encounter, an invoice or a payment.

**Direct business**: A sale or service received through a business's own channels, such as walk-in or phone, independently of marketplace discovery.

**Marketplace order**: A commercial request attributed to the Jansetu marketplace. Online payment alone does not establish marketplace attribution.

**Lab fulfilment mode**: The agreed way an assigned laboratory manages an order: provider API or Jansetu panel. Marketplace participation is a separate choice.

**Stock reservation**: A temporary allocation of identified units for a purpose; the units have not yet been dispensed or consumed.

**Stock movement**: A recorded change in the location, quantity or usability of stock, including receipt, dispensing, consumption, transfer or reversal.

**Report release**: An authorized laboratory's publication of an approved report version for permitted recipients. Clinician review is a separate care action.

**Clinical amendment**: An attributed correction or addition preserving the original clinical artifact and its history.

**Preliminary report**: A released or pending report whose test/result scope is incomplete or explicitly provisional. Its version and completeness remain distinguishable from a final or corrected report.

**Catalogue authority**: The authorized party maintaining shared product identity and sourced regulatory attributes. A business's local selling price or stock permission does not grant this authority.

**Quality release**: An authorized, evidenced decision allowing a restricted lot to become usable under applicable policy. A quantity correction or return does not by itself release the lot.

## Payments and phone access

**Invoice**: An issued statement of charges owed by a customer to its issuer.
_Avoid_: Using payment, receipt or settlement as synonyms.

**Receipt**: Evidence of an accepted collection, allocated to the appropriate customer balance or invoice.

**Credit note**: A document reducing a previously invoiced amount. It does not by itself prove that money was refunded.

**Settlement**: Reconciliation and discharge of an amount payable between parties. A calculated payable is distinct from a confirmed payout.

**Software subscription**: A business's plan for using Jansetu management software.
_Avoid_: Confusing this with a patient's recurring medicine order.

**Wallet credit**: An increase in the customer's balance held within the platform. It does not establish that funds returned through the original payment method.
_Avoid_: Calling every wallet credit a gateway refund.

**Gateway refund**: An evidenced return of collected funds through the payment provider, distinct from a wallet credit, credit note or stock return.

**Authorized payment**: A provider-confirmed authorization that has not necessarily been captured. It is distinct from a completed collection.

**Customer wallet liability**: The platform's obligation represented by a customer's recorded spendable wallet balance.

**Platform-business payable**: An amount the platform owes a business under the agreed collection and settlement arrangement.

**Payment intent**: A request to settle an identified charge with a specified payer and amount. It may have several attempts over time without becoming several charges.

**Payment attempt**: One tracked effort to complete a payment intent, with its own pending, confirmed or unresolved outcome.

**Wallet movement**: A specific change to a customer's wallet balance, such as a debit, credit or reversal. Repeated notification of the same movement is distinct from a new legitimate partial refund.

**Assisted top-up**: A recorded addition to a patient's balance through an authorized partner, with an identified payer, funding source, permitted uses and receipt. Consultation-only balance versus General patient wallet is an explicit funding-model decision; mobile-network airtime is separate.

**Phone consultation**: A clinical encounter reached through ordinary telephone calling, with patient identity, consent and the responsible practitioner recorded independently of the call's connection status.

**Consultation reservation**: Funds and service capacity temporarily held for an identified consultation; held funds are excluded from available spending. Waiting, unanswered calls and reconnect attempts are distinct from another chargeable clinical service.

**Partner-paid consultation**: A partner funds one named patient's consultation without creating an unspent patient balance for that operation.

**Consultation-only balance**: Reloadable patient credit restricted to approved consultation charges under the shared financial authority.

**General patient wallet**: A patient account whose explicitly approved spending uses extend beyond consultation-only credit.

**Independently verified contact**: A phone contact whose possession and connection to the identified patient or authorized caregiver have both been independently confirmed for the intended purpose. A saved number, successful call or collecting cashier's assertion alone is insufficient; shared-family use requires recorded authority.

**Refund obligation**: An evidenced amount owed back to a payer/customer after an approved reversal or cancellation, distinct from an actually completed wallet, gateway or cash return.
