## What must be expanded during low level design

For every command enumerate allowed and forbidden state transitions, actor/tenant/patient/branch scope, required/optional/null/unknown input, exact amount/unit boundaries, idempotency identity/window and immutable evidence. Test through every live writer, not just a newly added service. Include old/new clients, concurrency on two processes, duplicate/reordered/delayed callbacks, unknown external outcomes, crash points, partial fulfilment, reversal and restored-state replay.

Use property/state-machine tests for stock conservation, available funds and captured-source refund budgets, shared capacity and versioned clinical release. They complement named examples and remain constrained by approved business rules. No finite catalogue proves all inputs or interleavings safe; targeted assertions, runtime observability, controlled pilot and incident drills reduce residual uncertainty.

Before each phase freezes its schema, provide a field dictionary, relationship/cardinality model, indexes with duplicate/partial/null migration evidence, versioned API/event contracts, provider fixtures, command/transaction boundaries and a traceable test mapping. M0 documents current-flow hardening first; full IPD, disconnected sync, F14 holds and new billing policies are not new dependencies for M0 fixes.

## Release gates and deliberate deferral

1. **M0:** establish a reproducible toolchain/CI and real isolated tests; implement known current-flow security, wallet/payment/report and worker/index safety fixes with failing-before/passing-after regression evidence. Current verified gateway capture/hold policy is required; later billing policy decisions do not delay preservation of real funds/report evidence.
2. **Before each new workflow:** approve its applicable SR/D/P decisions and module contracts. Pass the in-scope security, patient-safety, stock/money/capacity and clinical-version scenarios; unresolved core-access, lost-money, stock corruption or patient-safety failures block that release. A skipped required case or missing assertion is not a pass. Do not substitute an arbitrary overall pass-rate or line-coverage percentage for these checks.
3. **Before pilot:** execute 10-20 synthetic cross-service journeys with named product, clinical, operations and technology/security owners. Include unlisted/private business, caregiver/no-phone care, counter retail/wholesale, API/panel lab, corrections, refund, partner failure and urgent/critical-result handover. Agree real support hours and recovery responsibility.
4. **Before capacity claims:** use the unchanged canonical 100 aggregate incoming HTTP RPS profile: 30-minute target run and then approved two-hour soak, transaction reconciliation, queue/worker work and controlled recovery. Provider sandboxes/stubs and real provider contract checks are reported separately. Set and measure phone concurrency/call costs independently.
5. **Before rollout:** retain deployed commit/build, fixture and contract versions, environment, per-case assertions, skip/block/failure reasons, reconciliation, latency/queue results and tested restore/rollback-forward plan. Plan a bounded pilot/canary and accountable monitoring; this review performs no deployment.

ABHA/ABDM onboarding remains optional and separately approved, disconnected sync remains M7, and advanced IPD/LIS/analyzer workflows remain separately scoped M8 work. Those scenarios are DEFERRED in this initial register, not accidentally forgotten. They require their own design/contract/safety tests before activation. Provider/ERP/library selection can wait only until its affected adapter/module design; identity and money invariants must be preserved now.

## Primary guidance and limits

Checked 9 November 2026. [Google SRE testing for reliability](https://sre.google/sre-book/testing-reliability/) distinguishes unit/integration/system/performance/regression checks and connects evidence to confidence; it does not justify a guarantee from a written list. [OWASP ASVS](https://asvs.owasp.org/) provides verifiable application-security requirements; the project still needs an explicitly scoped requirement-to-test assessment, not an unsupported compliance percentage. [Razorpay webhook guidance](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/best-practices/) documents duplicate and out-of-order delivery, supporting the replay/reconciliation cases above. The local catalogue is an engineering assessment, not certification by these sources.

No dependency install, application fix, schema migration, index sync, provider request, DB connection/drop, real message/call or production action was performed. No claim of an independent/blind architecture review, complete test coverage or a passing application suite is made. Complete module LLDs, approved policies, executable regression suites and staging/pilot evidence remain implementation work.
